Privacy Policy — Steer

Rippliq Studio

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Steer stores everything on your device.

We do not run accounts, we do not have a server, and your goals, blocks, reviews and reflections never leave your phone. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in this app.

Notifications are scheduled locally by your device. Photos you choose as an avatar are read from your gallery and stored only on this device.

If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, everything is gone permanently. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had it. That is the reason Steer has a backup: it is the only protection against a lost phone that does not require handing your data to somebody.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export, Steer writes a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes next is entirely your choice — a cloud drive, an email to yourself, a file manager. Steer does not upload it, does not know where you sent it, and never sees it again. Anything you send to a cloud service is then covered by that service's terms rather than by ours.

A backup file contains everything you have written, in plain text. Treat it the way you would treat a diary.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your card details. Google shares only what is needed to confirm that a subscription is active.

Children

Steer is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
